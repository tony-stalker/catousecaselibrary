Migration Planner

Describe the estate you are displacing and get a sequenced migration methodology assembled from this library — phases in dependency order, the prerequisites each one needs, the risks worth naming early, and the pages that carry the detail.

Internal tool — a starting point, not a deliverable

The plan is composed by fixed rules from the pages in this library, so two people entering the same estate get the same answer. It knows nothing about the customer beyond what you type, and nothing is saved anywhere but this browser. Read it, cut what does not apply, then own the result — it is a first draft of your thinking, not a substitute for it.
