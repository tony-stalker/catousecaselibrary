## Slide 2: Executive Summary
- CISOs are expected to deliver AI’s upside while ensuring downsides aren’t making headlines. Boards want growth, regulators want control, and customers want trust. A leaked prompt, a vulnerable model, or a noncompliant workflow will quickly turn an innovation story into a crisis.
- Cato Networks has created a Strategic AI Framework giving CISOs a blueprint to align AI adoption with enterprise security, modeled on best practices.
- The framework begins with Shadow AI Discovery and the Cato AI Security Visibility Assessment is offered at no cost to customers for a limited time.
- The capabilities required are native to the Cato SASE Cloud Platform. Enabling them will not cause disruption of business for <customer> during the assessment period. Cato Sales Engineers will provide the necessary expertise.
