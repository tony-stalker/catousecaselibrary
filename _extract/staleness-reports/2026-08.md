## Rows checked this cycle

### 1. EU AI Act Digital Omnibus — publication status (ai-eu-ai-act)
**Verdict: updated.** The page's "Read this first" callout and objective paragraph said
Official Journal publication of the omnibus amending regulation "was still pending as
this page was written." That changed since the July 2026 build: the omnibus was
published in the OJ as **Regulation (EU) 2026/1744** on **24 July 2026** and entered
into force on **27 July 2026** (three independent trackers — Hunton, NicFab,
lawandtechnology.eu — agree on the number and dates). The already-published deferred
dates on the page (Annex III high-risk incl. Art 26 → 2 Dec 2027; Annex I → 2 Aug 2028)
match what the final regulation actually enacted, so no figures changed — only the
"pending" framing needed correcting to "published." Edited both the callout and the
main paragraph to cite the regulation number and dates and drop "pending."

### 2. Annual Gartner MQ refresh — 2026 Gartner MQ for SASE Platforms (migration-cloudflare)
**Verdict: updated (partial).** A new **2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for SASE Platforms**
published around **28–31 July 2026** — after last month's review, which found no 2026
edition yet. Cato Networks' own press release (30/31 Jul 2026) confirms **Cato was named
a Leader for the third consecutive year**; other coverage (Netskope's own release,
ChannelFutures) independently confirms Netskope, Fortinet and Palo Alto Networks as the
other Leaders and Cisco/Versa as Challengers. Added a sentence noting Cato's repeat
Leader placement in the 2026 MQ, sourced to the press release.
**Could not confirm Cloudflare's specific category in the 2026 report** — the report
itself is paywalled and press coverage found so far only calls out the Leaders/

cycle.

## Notes / limitations

WebFetch returned HTTP 403 to almost every external site attempted this cycle (CISA.gov,
Cisco.com PR pages, EUR-Lex, dsptoolkit.nhs.uk, Gartner-adjacent press pages, cvefeed.io)
— consistent with the tooling limitation noted in July's report. Findings above rely on
WebSearch result snippets plus a small number of sites that did permit fetches (GitHub
raw content). A direct CISA KEV JSON pull via a GitHub mirror also proved unreliable —
the fetch tool's summarisation model appears to truncate large JSON files rather than
scanning the whole catalogue, returning implausibly low counts (3 Fortinet / 0 Sophos)
that were discarded in favour of the corroborated Senserva figures. Recommend a
browser-based or `curl`-based spot-check against the live CISA KEV catalogue next cycle,
and a manual check of the actual 2026 Gartner MQ document (behind Gartner's paywall or a
vendor-hosted reprint) to confirm Cloudflare's category before the next Cloudflare-page
touch.

run in this cloud session — run `bash _extract/verify.sh` locally before merging.
