Planner: Excel export, and a deck that packs to the space it has

  • Excel export writes a real .xlsx (same store-only ZIP as the .pptx): a tab
  • per heading — Summary, Plan, Prerequisites, Risks, Evidence, Use cases —

  • plan can leave the planner and become the tracker the migration is run
  • from. Frozen header row and autofilter on every tab.
  • The deck was 40+ slides because it chunked at a fixed six bullets and
  • half of every slide went to waste. Slides are now packed by measurement:
  • each section is emitted whole, then fitted against the real stage height
  • one column, then two, and only split when two will not hold it. Splits
  • are balanced so a full slide is not followed by a stub. A Summary/Full
  • control decides depth: Summary caps phase steps and renders
  • prerequisites, risks and evidence as where they concentrate rather than
  • listing every item — the document and workbook carry the record.
  • A worked estate goes from 40 slides to 16 with no continuations and no
  • overflow. verify.sh now measures overflow the way reflow does and fails
  • if the summary deck grows past 24 slides.
